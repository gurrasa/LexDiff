# FREELANCE SERVICES ESCROW AGREEMENT

**Date:** 1 October 2026

**Between**

1. **Acme Analytics AB** ("the Client"), and
2. **Jane Developer** ("the Freelancer").

## 1. Services
The Freelancer shall design and deliver a data dashboard as described in Schedule A (the "Deliverable").

## 2. Fee and escrow
2.1 The fee for the Services is **1,000 USDC** (one thousand USD Coin).
2.2 The Client shall deposit the full fee into the escrow smart contract designated by the parties before work starts. The deposit shall be held in escrow and may only be released or refunded in accordance with this Agreement.

## 3. Delivery
3.1 The Freelancer shall deliver the Deliverable within **thirty (30) days** of the deposit.

## 4. Review and payout
4.1 The Client has **seven (7) days** from delivery to review the Deliverable.
4.2 The escrowed fee shall be released to the Freelancer when the Client approves the Deliverable, or automatically if the Client has not responded when the review period ends.

## 5. Missed deadline
5.1 If the Freelancer fails to deliver within the period in clause 3.1, the Client is entitled to a full refund of the deposited fee from the escrow.

## 6. No discretionary control
6.1 No party, platform operator or contract administrator shall have the power to withdraw, redirect or freeze the escrowed funds other than as set out in clauses 4 and 5.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden.

*Signed by the Client and the Freelancer.*
