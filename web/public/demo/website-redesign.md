# WEBSITE REDESIGN ESCROW AGREEMENT

**Date:** 15 October 2026

**Between**

1. **Nordic Bakery AB** ("the Client"), and
2. **Pixel Studio HB** ("the Designer").

## 1. Services
The Designer shall redesign the Client's website as described in Schedule A.

## 2. Fee and escrow
2.1 The fee is **2,500 USDC** (two thousand five hundred USD Coin).
2.2 The Client shall deposit the full fee into the escrow smart contract before work starts.

## 3. Delivery
3.1 The Designer shall deliver within **fourteen (14) days** of the deposit.

## 4. Review and payout
4.1 The Client has **five (5) days** from delivery to review the work.
4.2 The fee is released to the Designer when the Client approves, or automatically when the review period ends without a response.

## 5. Missed deadline
5.1 If the Designer fails to deliver within the period in clause 3.1, the Client is entitled to a full refund.

## 6. Platform powers
6.1 The escrow platform operator may **pause** the escrow while a dispute between the parties is being resolved.
6.2 Apart from clause 6.1, no party or operator may change the terms, deadlines or amounts of this escrow, or withdraw the escrowed funds.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden.
