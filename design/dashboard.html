How LexDiff works

  1. The lawyer and client sign the agreement and its terms.
  2. Chainlink checks the deployed contract against every term.
  3. Each failing property is fixed or knowingly accepted, with a signature.
  4. A fix counts only when Chainlink sees the property pass.

LexDiff preserves evidence; it does not decide liability or give legal advice. Sepolia testnet only.
